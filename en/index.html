02
Thermal & infrared

Thermal Imaging Diagnostics

Our professional thermal camera reveals what happens behind plaster, tiles and fresh paint: moisture, leaks, missing insulation, overloaded electrics – completely non-destructive, without opening a single wall.

Leak detection: locating burst & leaking pipes
Tracing moisture in walls, ceilings & floors
Spotting mould-risk zones before they become visible
Insulation check: gaps & heat loss
Thermal bridges on façades, ceilings & shutter boxes
Draughty windows & doors: failed seals exposed
Flat roof & waterproofing inspection
Underfloor heating: layout & function made visible
Locating hot & cold water pipes inside walls
Electrical check: sockets, fuse box & overheating contacts
Air conditioning & boiler: performance & energy loss
Before/after documentation for renovations

Worth knowing: a fresh coat of paint costs a few hundred euros – an overlooked water problem quickly costs a hundred times more. Thermal imaging shows the truth beneath the paint.

How the price scales with property size

The prices shown apply to the floor area stated on each package. Larger properties simply take more time on site – so we scale fairly and transparently:

  • up to 80 m²Essential Check from €179 · Full Check from €299
  • 80–130 m²Full Check from €299 · Premium 360° from €429
  • 130–200 m²surcharge of approx. €80 per additional 50 m²
  • 200–350 m² (villa)Full Check from €490 · Premium 360° from €690
  • over 350 m² · apartment blockindividual fixed-price quote

Example: a 300 m² villa takes roughly 6–8 hours instead of 2–4 – so the Full Check there starts at €490 rather than €299. You learn this up front, not afterwards: send us the property type, approximate m² and location and you get a binding fixed price before you decide. We never add surcharges later.

Construction monitoring as a subscription: for new builds we offer recurring visits with photos, a 360° tour and a short report – priced by frequency and property size, ideally as a monthly package. Just ask.

All prices are “from” prices depending on property size and location – you receive your fixed price before booking. Individual services (e.g. leak detection only, or measurement only) on request as well. Travel within the Antalya/Alanya region included.
