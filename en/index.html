What is a thermal camera, actually?

It makes temperature differences visible. That sounds technical, but it is simple: a damp wall is cooler than a dry one. On the thermal image, the wet area shows up as a dark shadow — even if the wall was freshly painted and looks perfectly dry to you.

The same way we find leaking pipes behind tiles, missing insulation in the façade and overheating cables in the fuse box. All without drilling a single hole.
